Walker Birchfield

Creative Director · Photography & Design

Hello! My name is Walker Birchfield and I am excited to share the creative side of my work with you. I pride myself on being a “jack of all trades” and love to bring different topics and ideas into all of my bodies of work.

My main bodies of creative works include photography, graphic design, videography, digital media, and creative content with most of those areas typically revolving around my interest in sports. In addition, computer programming, information technology, and home networking are other creative areas that I frequently deal with.

I graduated from Purdue University in May 2026 with a bachelors degree in History, a minor in Computer and Information Technology, and a certificate in Cornerstone. During my time at Purdue I worked for Purdue Athletics in the Ross-Ade Brigade, the official student section for Purdue Football, where I served as the Creative Director. In the summer I spent my time working for the Lafayette Aviators, a summer collegiate baseball team, also serving as Creative Director.

Walker Birchfield

Juris Doctor Candidate · Indiana University McKinney School of Law

Hello, my name is Walker Birchfield and welcome to the professional side of my portfolio. I graduated from Purdue University in May 2026 with a bachelor’s degree in History, a minor in Information Technology, and a certificate in Cornerstone. Throughout my time there, I developed a passion for research, writing, digital media production, and sports which helped shape my journey and where I wanted to take my career.

After graduating from Purdue I began my law journey at Indiana University–McKinney School of Law where I am currently a 1L student. I entered law school with a focus on sports law stemming from my previous work under Purdue Athletics where I served as a Creative Director for the Ross-Ade Brigade. While my main task was designing promotions and creating graphics, in the background I assisted in sponsorship negotiations and contracts for the purchase of thousands of promotional items. Currently, I am a member of the Sports and Entertainment Law Society at IU–McKinney where I work alongside other students who share the same interest in the business and legal side of athletics.

My goal is to combine my legal education with my background in sports to keep working in athletics with an emphasis on sports law and player representation.
