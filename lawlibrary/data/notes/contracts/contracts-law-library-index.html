Map of content

Study materials for Contracts (Professor Lourie, Fall 2026; final exam December 4, 2026, 4 hours, closed book, multiple choice and essays; the final is 90% of the grade). The library has 93 case briefs (every case named in the slides), 27 topic notes, and three study documents. Casebook: Knapp et al., Problems in Contract Law (10th ed. 2023). Items marked [Outside knowledge] come from outside the course materials.

Conventions

  • Case briefs (type: case) all use the same sections: Overview, Parties, Facts, Procedural History, Issue, Holding, Reasoning, Rule, Why It Matters, Other Key Points, Sources.
  • Topic notes (type: topic) open with a one-paragraph summary callout, then rules and elements, an exam framework, hypos from the slides, a key-cases table and sources.
  • Filenames have no trailing periods, ampersands or apostrophes (for example, Jacob and Youngs v. Kent).
  • Unverified details are marked [Unverified] or [Outside knowledge].
