Quick reference

Citation: 68 N.Y.2d 373, 509 N.Y.S.2d 507, 502 N.E.2d 184 (1986) · Casebook: cited in Oppenheimer (Knapp pp. 832-833) and Oppenheimer Note 3 (p. 835) · Topic: Express Conditions › Strict compliance One line: Written notice of cancellation that arrived a few days late did not satisfy an express condition, even though the other party already knew the buyers intended to cancel.

Coverage

Known from Oppenheimer's citation and Note 3, and from the slides. Facts beyond those are [Outside knowledge].

Overview

A strict-compliance case: an express condition about the form and timing of notice is enforced literally, even when actual notice was received.

Parties

  • Plaintiff: Maxton Builders, Inc., the seller and builder of a house. [Outside knowledge]
  • Defendants: the Lo Galbos, buyers who tried to cancel the contract. [Outside knowledge]

Facts

  • Slides: a written notice of cancellation arrived a few days late. The other party clearly knew of the buyers' intent to cancel.
  • [Outside knowledge]: the contract allowed the buyers to cancel by written notice by a certain date. They told the seller by phone on time, but the written notice was mailed so that it arrived late.

Procedural History

[Outside knowledge] The New York Court of Appeals held for the seller (the cancellation was ineffective).

Issue

Is an express condition requiring written notice by a deadline satisfied by actual knowledge or late written notice?

Holding

No. The express condition was not satisfied. The attempted cancellation failed.

Reasoning

  • Express conditions must be literally performed. If the parties wanted a different arrangement, "the time to say so [was] at the bargaining table" (quoted in Oppenheimer, Knapp p. 833).

Rule

Where a contract makes timely written notice an express condition, actual knowledge or late notice does not satisfy it.

Why It Matters

  • It supports Oppenheimer's strict-compliance rule.
  • Slides: the attorney on the losing side was held liable for malpractice, a practical warning to calendar condition deadlines and follow required notice formalities exactly.
