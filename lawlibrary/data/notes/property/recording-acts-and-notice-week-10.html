Recording Act Statutes (Week 10)

A. Types of Recording Acts

  1. Notice Statute:
    • Rule: A subsequent Bona Fide Purchaser (BFP) for value without notice prevails over a prior unrecorded conveyance, regardless of who records first.
  2. Race-Notice Statute (e.g., Indiana IC 32-21-4-1):
    • Rule: A subsequent BFP for value without notice prevails ONLY if BFP records first before the prior grantee.
  3. Pure Race Statute:
    • Rule: The grantee who records first wins title, regardless of notice or BFP status.

Forms of Legal Notice (Week 10)

  1. Actual Notice: Personal knowledge of prior unrecorded transfer.
  2. Constructive Notice: Legal notice imparted by proper recording of deed in chain of title.
  3. Inquiry Notice: Notice charged to buyer based on physical inspection of land (e.g., tenant in possession) or facts cited in title documents requiring further investigation.

The Shelter Rule (Week 10)

  • Rule: A transferee who takes property from a BFP receives the same recording act protection as the BFP grantor, even if the transferee had actual/constructive notice of a prior unrecorded interest or received property as a gift.
  • Purpose: Protects the BFP's right to freely alienate and transfer property.
