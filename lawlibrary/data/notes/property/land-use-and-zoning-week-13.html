A. Four Historical Forces Driving Land Use Planning

  1. Industrialization & conflict over land uses.
  2. Population growth and urban demographic shifts.
  3. Transportation & skyscraper building technology.
  4. Confidence in government municipal planning.

B. Nonconforming Uses & Vested Rights

  • Vested Rights Doctrine: Protects planned land developments before construction if developer made substantial commitments/expenditures in good faith.
  • Estoppel: Applied when developer relies reasonably to their detriment on an issued government permit (Northwestern Distributors v. Zoning Board).
  • Amortization: Provides nonconforming businesses a graceful period to conform or relocate after zoning changes.
