Every real estate sales contract carries an implied covenant that the seller will deliver marketable title at closing.

  • Definition: Title reasonably free from doubt, unrecorded encumbrances, or threat of litigation.
  • Defects Making Title Unmarketable:
    1. Encumbrances (unrecorded mortgages, liens, servitudes not excepted in contract).
    2. Defects in chain of title (e.g., missing grantor, unresolved adverse possession claim).
    3. Existing zoning violations on the property.
  • Timing: Covenant applies at closing. Seller must be given opportunity to cure defects before closing.
