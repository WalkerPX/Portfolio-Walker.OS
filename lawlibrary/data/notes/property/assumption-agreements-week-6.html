• Assumption Agreement: Occurs when an Assignee or Subtenant expressly promises in writing to assume and perform all tenant covenants in the prime lease.
  • Third-Party Beneficiary (3PB) Enforcement:
    • If Subtenant (S) enters into an assumption contract with T, Landlord (L) acquires rights as an intended 3rd Party Beneficiary.
    • If S breaches rent payment and T fails to sue, L can sue S directly under P/K as a third-party beneficiary.
