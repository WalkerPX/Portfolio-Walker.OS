A. General Warranty Deed

  • Language Cue: "warrants and will defend title against lawful claims of all persons"
  • Liability Scope: Grantor guarantees title against all defects, whether arising before or during grantor's ownership.

B. Special (Limited) Warranty Deed

  • Language Cue: "warrants and will defend against claims by, through, or under Grantor"
  • Liability Scope: Grantor guarantees title ONLY against defects created during grantor's period of ownership.

C. Quitclaim Deed

  • Language Cue: "conveys and quitclaims any and all interest"
  • Liability Scope: Zero warranties. Grantor conveys whatever interest they hold; grantee bears all title defect risk.
