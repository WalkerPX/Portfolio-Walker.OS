The executory period spans the signing of the sales contract to the formal closing.

A. Doctrine of Equitable Conversion

  • Legal Ownership: Once a valid, enforceable sales contract is executed:
    • Buyer acquires Equitable Title (real property interest).
    • Seller retains Legal Title solely as security for the unpaid purchase price (personal property interest).

B. Risk of Loss (Casualty Loss)

  • Majority / Common Law Rule: Risk of loss from casualty (e.g., fire, storm) passes to the buyer upon contract execution because buyer holds equitable title.
  • Minority Rule (Uniform Vendor & Purchaser Risk Act): Risk of loss remains with seller until buyer receives legal title or physical possession.
