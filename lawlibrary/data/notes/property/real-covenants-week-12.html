A real covenant is a promise respecting land use that binds successors.

A. Requirements for Burden to Run to Successor

  1. Writing: Satisfies Statute of Frauds.
  2. Intent: Original parties intended burden to bind successors.
  3. Touch and Concern: Promise directly affects physical use or value of land.
  4. Horizontal Privity: Privity of estate between original covenanting parties (grantor-grantee relationship at time covenant created).
  5. Strict Vertical Privity: Successor takes full legal estate of original covenantor.
  6. Notice: Successor had actual, constructive, or inquiry notice.

B. Requirements for Benefit to Run to Successor

  1. Writing, Intent, Touch & Concern.
  2. Relaxed Vertical Privity: Successor takes same or lesser estate. Horizontal Privity is NOT required for benefit to run.
