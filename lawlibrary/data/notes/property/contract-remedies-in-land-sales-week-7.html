A. Expectation Damages (Benefit of the Bargain)

  • General Formula: $ ext{Damages} = ext{Contract Price} - ext{Fair Market Value at Time of Breach}$.
  • Measures the promised economic position against market reality.

B. Rescission and Restitution

  • Unwinds the transaction. Seller retains deposit upon buyer's breach; buyer recovers deposit upon seller's breach.

C. Buyer's and Seller's Liens

  • Buyer's Lien: Equitably attaches to seller's property to secure return of earnest money deposit upon seller's breach.
  • Seller's Lien: Secures unpaid purchase price.

D. Specific Performance

  • Equitable remedy ordering conveyance of title, grounded in the legal presumption that every parcel of land is unique.
