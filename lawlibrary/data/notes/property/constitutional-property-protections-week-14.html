A. Constitutional Structure

  • Articles I, II, III: Establish Federal Legislative, Executive, and Judicial structures.
  • Fifth Amendment Takings Clause: "nor shall private property be taken for public use, without just compensation."

B. Constitutional Property Clauses

Constitutional ProvisionLegal Impact on Land Use & Property Rights
Fifth Amendment Takings ClauseBars uncompensated government physical takings or regulatory takings of private property.
Due Process (5th & 14th Amendments)Protects property owners against arbitrary, irrational, or retroactive land use regulations.
Equal Protection (14th Amendment)Prohibits discriminatory municipal zoning enforcement or racially discriminatory land classifications.
