Introduction to Present Estates & Future Interests (Week 2)

A. Fee Simple Absolute (FSA)

  • Nature: Absolute ownership of unlimited duration. Endures forever unless conveyed away.
  • Common Law Form: "to A and his heirs"
  • Modern Form: "to A" (presumes transfer of full fee simple).

B. Life Estate (LE)

  • Nature: Present possessory interest measured by the duration of a human life.
  • Creation: "to A for life"
  • Future Interests:
    • Reversion in Grantor (O) if no third party named.
    • Remainder in Third Party (B) if specified ("to A for life, then to B").

C. Life Estate Pur Autre Vie

  • Nature: A life estate measured by the life of a person other than the holder ("for the life of another").
  • Creation: "to A for the life of B". If A dies before B, A's interest passes to A's heirs/devisees until B dies.

Future Interests Retained by Grantor (O) (Week 3)

A. Reversion

  • Definition: A future interest left in O when O conveys a lesser durational estate than O originally owned (e.g., life estate, term of years).
  • Vested Status: Reversions are automatically vested from the time of creation.
  • Transferability: Fully alienable inter vivos, devisable by will, and inheritable by intestate succession.
  • Becoming Possessory: Automatically becomes possessory upon the natural expiration of the prior estate.

B. Possibility of Reverter

  • Definition: Future interest retained by O when conveying a Fee Simple Determinable (FSD).
  • Mechanism: Automatically reverts to O immediately upon the occurrence of the stated limitation event.

C. Right of Entry (Power of Termination)

  • Definition: Future interest retained by O when conveying a Fee Simple Subject to Condition Subsequent (FSSCS).
  • Mechanism: Does NOT revert automatically. O must take affirmative legal action or physical re-entry to terminate the estate once the condition is broken.

Practice Problem Scenarios (Week 3)

  • Grant: "O to A for life, then to B for life."
    • If O dies testate devising property to C, then A dies, then B dies:
    • Immediately after grant: A has LE; B has Remainder in LE; O has Reversion in FSA.
    • Upon O's death: C receives O's Reversion in FSA.
    • Upon A's death: B's LE becomes possessory.
    • Upon B's death: C holds Fee Simple Absolute.
