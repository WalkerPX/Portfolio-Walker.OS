A. Benefits of Zoning Regulations

  • Separation of incompatible land uses (e.g., keeping heavy industry away from residential housing).
  • Promotes predictability, stable property values, and community character.

B. Societal Costs of Zoning Regulations

  • Exclusionary Zoning: Single-family zoning excludes lower-income households.
  • Increases housing prices and complicates multi-family (e.g., duplex) residential development.
  • Fosters economic and racial segregation.
