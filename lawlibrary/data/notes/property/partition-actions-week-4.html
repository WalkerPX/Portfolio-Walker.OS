When co-owners cannot agree on land use or management, any joint tenant or tenant in common has an absolute right to seek court-ordered partition to end the co-ownership.

Partition RemedyLegal MechanismFactual Conditions for Order
Partition in KindPhysical division of the property into separately owned parcels.Default / Preferred Remedy. Applied when land can be divided fairly and practicably, and separate parcels represent equal relative value.
Partition by SaleForced public/private sale of property; net proceeds divided proportionally.Ordered ONLY when physical division is impracticable (e.g., single-family home) or evidence proves sale better promotes owners' interests.
