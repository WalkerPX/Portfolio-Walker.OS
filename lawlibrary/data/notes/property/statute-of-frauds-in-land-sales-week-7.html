To be legally enforceable, a contract for the sale of real property must satisfy the Statute of Frauds.

A. Mandatory SoF Requirements

  1. Written Instrument: Document evidencing contract.
  2. Signature: Signed by the party to be charged (party being sued).
  3. Property Description: Reasonably identifies the real estate conveyed.
  4. Essential Terms: States essential terms with reasonable certainty (identifies parties, purchase price/payment method, closing date, deed type).

B. Dan's Message Application (Lazy Daisy Cottage)

  • An informal message expressing desire to sell property at an appraised price of $425,000 leaves essential transaction terms open (closing date, payment terms, formal offer acceptance), failing SoF enforceability without further written assent.
