1. Listing Agreement: Seller engages a real estate broker/agent to market property. Broker owes fiduciary duties of loyalty, care, and disclosure to seller.
  2. Sales Contract (Purchase & Sale Agreement): Bilateral executory contract allocating terms, conditions, inspection contingencies, and closing date.
  3. Closing: Execution and delivery of deed, tender of purchase price, and title transfer.
  4. Post-Closing: Sales contract covenants merge into deed; legal title disputes governed by deed warranties.
