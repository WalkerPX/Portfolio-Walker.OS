Successive adverse possessors can combine their periods of possession to satisfy the SOL through the doctrine of tacking.

A. Requirement of Privity

Tacking is allowed only if there is privity of estate between the successive adverse possessors. Privity represents a voluntary transfer of the possessory interest.

B. Valid Privity Transfers

  • Deed / Written Conveyance
  • Will or Intestate Succession
  • Valid oral transfer of possession (in limited circumstances)

C. Ouster Exception

If AP2 forcibly ousts AP1 without a voluntary transfer, privity does NOT exist, and AP2 cannot tack AP1's time.
