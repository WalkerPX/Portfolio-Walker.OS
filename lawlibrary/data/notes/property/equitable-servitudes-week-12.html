A. Requirements for Burden to Run

  1. Writing (or implied from common subdivision scheme).
  2. Intent: Original parties intended restriction to bind successors.
  3. Touch and Concern: Restricts land use or affects land value.
  4. Notice: Successor took with Actual, Constructive, or Inquiry notice.
  5. NO PRIVITY REQUIRED! Neither Horizontal nor Vertical privity is needed to enforce an equitable servitude.

B. Common Scheme & Implied Reciprocal Negative Servitudes

  • Where a developer sells lots in a residential subdivision under a uniform development scheme, negative covenants bind all retained and conveyed lots if buyers had notice of the scheme.
