• Function: Public registry to record documents, preserve title records, and protect purchasers against prior unrecorded WRITTEN transfers.
  • Critical Limitation: Recording acts do NOT guarantee title.
  • "Holes" in Recording System: Interests arising by operation of law are valid without being recorded:
    • Title by Adverse Possession
    • Oral short-term leases
    • Prescriptive easements & implied easements
    • Equitable servitudes & mechanic's liens
