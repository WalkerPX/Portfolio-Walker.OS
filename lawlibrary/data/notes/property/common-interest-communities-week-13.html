Enforceability of CIC Rules (Nahrstedt Standard)

  • Master association restrictions recorded in CC&Rs are presumptively valid.
  • Enforced unless restriction is arbitrary, violates public policy, or imposes burdens grossly disproportionate to community benefits (Nahrstedt v. Lakeside Village).
